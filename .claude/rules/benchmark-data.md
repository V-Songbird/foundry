# Research and benchmark evidence

The owner chose public research. Foundry's central documentation may publish
original investigations, methodology, limitations and reproducible results.
Do not apply the retired blanket rule that all results or reference names must
remain private. Sources should be identified and comparisons should be fair.

Publication still distinguishes original analysis and appropriate attributed
source material from account credentials, copied authentication homes, local
session state and disposable maintenance output. Those belong outside the
distributed tree, normally in .scratch/ or the runner's isolated temporary area.

Preserve recorded model, revision, date, input and measurement provenance.
Do not replace missing results with estimates, relabel one host's results as
another host's, or overwrite old evidence to make it support a new claim.
Use an explicit successor/correction when a record needs replacement.

Plugin installs should contain the runtime and relevant product documentation.
Benchmark tooling and substantial research belong in Foundry. Publication and
paid model runs remain distinct operations; an organization task authorizes
neither a new measurement nor an installation by itself.
